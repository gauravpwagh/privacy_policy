Move Minute privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Move Minute is a movement-break app for your workday and your commute. It is built to work entirely on your device. We do not collect any data.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings: working hours, workdays, reminder interval, lunch break, ride times and length, sound and theme choices.
  • Your history: the routines you play, when, for how long, and the optional "How did that feel?" rating.
  • A log of reminder outcomes (started, snoozed, skipped), used only to show how reliably reminders arrive.

This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • No network connection: the app makes no internet requests.
  • No camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you when a pause is due and when your ride starts. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Alarms and reminders (Android): so reminders arrive on time. If you decline, they may arrive a few minutes late.
  • Run at startup (Android): to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for quiet nudges on the train and during a routine.

Health information

Move Minute offers general wellness movement. It isn't medical advice or treatment, and it does not collect health data.

Children

Move Minute is intended for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
